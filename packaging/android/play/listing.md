# Inkline on Google Play: the store listing

What to paste into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing (docs/publishing.md has the
rest of the steps). Each section below is one field; tests/test_android.py checks they fit Play's limits (title 30
characters, short description 80, full description 4000). The graphics come from `make_graphics.py` (or the Android
workflow's **play-graphics** download), the screenshots from its **play-screenshots** download.

## Title
Inkline: Printable Pages

## Short description
Colouring pages, mazes, puzzles and worksheets to print, made up or from photos

## Full description
Inkline makes black-and-white pages for printing: colouring pages, puzzles, classroom worksheets and little books, made up fresh every time or made from your own photos.

MAKE ONE UP
• Mandala colouring pages, woven tiles, topographic contours and flow lines
• Mazes: rectangular, or shaped like a heart, a star, a fish, a house, or any word or name
• Dot-to-dots, word searches and spot the difference, with answer pages
• Sudoku, kakuro and nonograms, each with exactly one solution
• Name pages in bubble letters, and colour-by-number pages with a crayon key

CLASSROOM WORKSHEETS
• Maths drills (adding, taking away, times tables, number bonds, money), fractions, number lines and telling the time
• Handwriting to trace, crosswords, unscramble sheets, spelling tests and reading comprehension, each with an answer page
• Early maths with pictures for ages 4 to 6: ten frames, number bonds and counting
• Bingo cards, a different card for every child, with a caller's sheet
• A worksheet pack for the whole week, set for the age you choose, with a name line and answers
• Grid, dot, lined and handwriting paper, sticker charts and certificates with each child's name

FROM YOUR PHOTOS
• Turn a photo into a colouring page (a line drawing), colour by number, halftone dots, engraving lines, stippling or a nonogram puzzle that reveals the picture
• Rotate, zoom and crop before converting
• Make a colouring book from several photos, one page each, with a cover

BOOKLETS AND POSTERS
• Booklets of any pattern with a cover, page numbers, difficulty that rises page by page and answer pages
• Fold-and-staple books: two half-size pages per side, double-sided, that fold into a real little book
• Posters printed big across several sheets, with trim marks for putting them together

EASY TO PRINT
• Print straight to your printer through Android's print service, or save a PDF or PNG
• Letter or A4, with the page shown exactly as it will print
• "Easier to read" text in Atkinson Hyperlegible, made for readers with low vision
• In English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Italian and Dutch

Inkline is free and open source (GPL-3.0). It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it collects no data: everything you make stays on your device. It works offline; the only time it uses the internet is if you let it check GitHub for a new version.

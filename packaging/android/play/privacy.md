# Inkline privacy policy

Inkline is a free, open-source app for making pages to print. This policy covers Inkline on Android (from Google
Play or the APKs on GitHub) and on desktop.

## What Inkline collects

Nothing. Inkline has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it doesn't collect,
store on any server, sell or share any personal or device information.

## Your photos and files

Photos you open, the pages you make, and your projects and settings stay on your device. They are read only when
you choose them, and saved only where you choose. When you print, the pages go to Android's print service and the
printer you pick there, as with any app that prints.

## The one network request: checking for updates

The first time Inkline starts, it asks whether it may check for a new version. Only if you say yes, it asks GitHub
(`api.github.com`, the site where Inkline is published) for the latest release, at most once a day. That request
carries nothing about you or your work; like any web request, GitHub sees your device's IP address, under
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
You can turn it off at any time under Help → Check for updates automatically. If you say no, Inkline never uses the
internet.

## Children

Inkline is suitable for use with children, and since it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from
children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here, with the app's source code:
<https://github.com/Gshoe2006/inkline>. Questions: open an issue there, or write to the email address on Inkline's
Google Play listing.

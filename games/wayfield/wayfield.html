Wayfield

A city builder without a grid. Draw roads at any angle or curve, and land splits into parcels of whatever shape fits along them. Each building is generated to fit its parcel.

Getting started

  1. Draw streets off the highway that links you to the region. Curves bend lots into fans and wedges.
  2. Zone the parcels with the brush: residential, commercial and industrial.
  3. Build a wind turbine and a water tower beside a connected road. Power and water travel along the roads.
  4. Watch the demand bars. People need jobs, shops need customers, and services raise land value so buildings level up.
